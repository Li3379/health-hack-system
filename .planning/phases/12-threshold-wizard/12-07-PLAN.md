<objective>
Build `ThresholdWizard.vue`: the three-step guided configuration dialog that replaces four naked number inputs.

Purpose: This is the user-facing answer to 我点击添加后不知道怎么填. Every field explains itself in plain Chinese with its unit, the recommended value is pre-filled and labelled, a live zone bar shows where the input lands, a summary sentence states the resulting alert behaviour, and invalid orderings are blocked with two inline messages instead of a red toast.
Output: A self-contained wizard dialog component with its own test suite, driven entirely by metadata and the pure validator.
</objective>
