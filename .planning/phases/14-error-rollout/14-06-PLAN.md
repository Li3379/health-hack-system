<objective>
Wire the two remaining pages that have a genuine server-side field-error path and no ambiguity in field names, while handling the one page in this phase where two forms coexist. This plan implements D-01's user-visible-path rule (the two client-side pre-checks stay as they are, for the reason recorded in the ledger).

`user/Profile.vue` is the interesting case: it hosts a profile form and a password form side by side. A single composable instance would make the password form's summary and inline errors appear inside the personal-info card, and vice versa — the summary would list fields from the other form. Two instances, two field orders, two refs, is not a stylistic choice; it is the only way the two summaries can each answer "how many fields are wrong here".

It also carries ERR-05's most damaging failure mode: a rejected password change that clears the inputs forces the user to retype a password (and a wrong one is exactly what the server just rejected). The snapshot assertion in this plan's spec is the guard.

`screening/List.vue` carries a mixed file: two of its four call sites are client-side pre-validation (`file` type and size) and are not API failures, while the other two (load, upload, delete) are. Contract 6.2 requires the distinction be recorded as `保持` with a reason rather than silently migrated, because converting a "file too large" message into a `showError` call would misclassify a client check as a server failure and lose the specific copy.

Purpose: reach the point where every form on the app that can receive `fieldErrors` renders them inline, with the two-form case handled correctly.

Output: both pages fully wired, two specs pinning the two-form independence and the preservation property, and a nine-row ledger fragment including the explicit 保持 decisions.
</objective>
