<objective>
Extend the risk-assessment response with the two disclosure fields, capturing assumptions at the real substitution sites and recomputing them on read from the current profile.

Purpose: PRF-05 applies to the risk response as well as the score response. RESEARCH Pitfall 7 established that `risk_assessment` has no assumption columns and `schema.sql` only runs on an empty volume, so persisting the assumptions would need a migration this phase deliberately avoids. The RESOLVED decision is read-time recomputation: the badge means 「当前档案下，该评估所依赖的默认假设」. PRF-08's backfill panel lists exactly these sentences, so this plan also supplies the copy the panel will render.

Output: The extended `RiskAssessmentVO` record, collector-aware `RiskRuleEngine`, `toVO` recomputation, and `RiskAssessmentAssumptionsTest` covering all three reachable substitution paths.
</objective>
