<objective>
Turn the risk page into the place where an incomplete profile is disclosed, explained and optionally repaired in place — without ever blocking assessment creation.

Purpose: PRF-08 requires the page to show what will be estimated and let the user backfill the minimum required fields on the spot, while D-06 forbids a hard block. PRF-06 requires the 基于不完整档案估算 · 完善档案 badge on both result pages. The trigger order is not cosmetic: UI-SPEC Contract 5 and Contract 8 both say assumptions are evaluated before the score, because a panel with an empty list gives the user no reason to backfill.

Two correctness properties decide the shape of this plan. First, the store must actually hold a payload before the three-order rule runs (plan-check B1/B1-R): the page awaits the fetch in `onMounted` and awaits it again at click time when the payload is still absent, so a first visit behaves like a refresh instead of silently skipping the panel and the badge — and because 13-05's `fetchCompleteness()` returns the in-flight promise rather than short-circuiting, that click-time await genuinely waits even mid-mount. Second, the panel's list must be the list the engine will apply (plan-check B2): it renders `completeness.assumptions`, which `ProfileFieldCatalog.assumptionsFor` fills with the same branches the risk engine uses, rather than joining `fields[].assumption` — that join emitted three sentences for a null profile while the engine applies one generic sentence.

D-06 also names 身高体重可选补, so the panel carries two optional inputs in addition to the two required ones (plan-check W5: the previous draft narrowed D-06 to two fields without recording it).

Output: `prevention/Risk.vue` with the badge, the panel (2 required + 2 optional inputs), the three actions and the detail-dialog assumption list, plus `RiskBackfill.spec.ts`.
</objective>
